# Sign in to Eufy

Eufy doesn't offer a "Sign in with Eufy" button for other apps, so the app signs in the same way the Eufy Security app does: with your Eufy email address, password and, the first time, a code Eufy emails you.

## Which account to use

**A second Eufy account works best.** Create one with another email address in the Eufy Security app, then share your doorbell and cameras with it from the Eufy Security app on your phone. Signing in on the TV with your main account can sometimes sign your phone out.

**Google or Apple sign-in:** if you sign in to Eufy with Google or Apple, your account has no password and can't be used here. Create a second account with an email address and password as above, and share your cameras with it.

## Sign in from your phone (easiest)

1. In the app, select **Eufy account**. A QR code appears on the TV.
2. Scan it with your phone's camera. Your phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as the TV.
3. Enter your Eufy email, password and country on the page that opens. You can paste from your password manager.
4. If Eufy emails you a verification code, type it on the TV (or on your phone if the page asks).

The page only works on your home network, works once, and closes after 5 minutes. Your details are encrypted on your phone with a one-time key from the QR code before they're sent to the TV.

## Sign in with the remote

Type your details in the boxes next to the QR code instead. The Google TV app on your phone has a keyboard that types straight into the TV, which is quicker than the on-screen keyboard.

## Verification codes and picture checks

- **Verification code:** Eufy sends one to your email the first time the TV signs in. Type it in to finish.
- **Picture check:** sometimes Eufy shows a picture of characters to check you're a person. Type what you see.

## Where your password goes

Your password stays on the TV, encrypted with a key held in Android's secure key storage, and is only sent to Eufy. It is never sent to this project or anyone else. **Sign out** in the app deletes it.

## If Eufy signs you out later

If Eufy ends the session or asks for a new code, a red message appears on screen and repeats until you sign in again. Open the app and select **Eufy account** to fix it. You can change how often the reminder repeats in **Advanced**.
