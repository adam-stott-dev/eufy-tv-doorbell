# Settings

Open **Eufy TV Doorbell** from the apps row. Changes apply straight away.

## Status

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Watch for doorbell and motion | Turns the app on or off. |
| Show over other apps | Whether the pop-up can show on top of everything. See [Install](install.md#3-let-it-show-over-other-apps). |
| Battery and standby | Whether the app is excluded from battery saving. |
| Show a test pop-up | Shows the pop-up now with your current settings. |
| Show a test pop-up in 15 seconds | Gives you time to open another app or start the screensaver first. |

## Eufy account

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Eufy account | Sign in, see the connection status, or sign out. |
| Cameras that pop up | Choose which doorbells and cameras can pop up. |
| Show live view now | Opens a camera's live view without waiting for an event. |
| Keep video on my home network | On by default. Turn off to allow Eufy's relay when a direct connection to the HomeBase fails. |

## When to pop up

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Doorbell rings | On | Pop up when someone presses the doorbell. |
| Motion detected | On | Pop up when a camera sees motion. |
| Person detected | On | Pop up when a camera sees a person. |
| Show | Over everything | Or only when nothing is playing. |
| Wait between motion pop-ups | 10 seconds | Stops repeated motion events from popping up again and again. |
| Wake the TV and close the screensaver | On | Wakes the screen for a pop-up. |

## Quiet hours

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Use quiet hours | Off | No pop-ups between the times below. |
| From / Until | 23:00 to 07:00 | The quiet period. |
| Still show doorbell rings | On | Rings still pop up during quiet hours; motion doesn't. |

## Pop-up

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Style | Picture-in-picture | Or full screen. |
| Picture-in-picture size | Medium | Small, medium or large. |
| Picture-in-picture position | Top right | Any corner. |
| Seconds on screen | 30 | From 5 to 120 seconds. |
| Stay up while motion continues | On | Keeps the pop-up open while the camera still sees motion. |
| Show camera name and time | On | A label on the video. |
| Back button closes the pop-up | On | While the pop-up shows, Back closes it instead of going back in the app behind it. |
| Sound | Only for doorbell rings | Or always on, or off. |

## Advanced

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Remind me on screen when something is wrong | Every 30 minutes | How often a problem message repeats until it's fixed. |
| Stay connected while the TV sleeps | On | Faster pop-ups after standby, for a little more power. |
| Version | | The installed version. Compare it with the [latest release](https://github.com/adam-stott-dev/eufy-tv-doorbell/releases/latest). |
