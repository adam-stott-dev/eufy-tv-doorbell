# Settings

Open **Eufy TV Doorbell** from the apps row. Changes apply straight away. The main screen has the essentials; everything else is in the menus below it. Press Back to leave a menu.

## Main screen

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Watch for doorbell and motion | Turns the app on or off. |
| Eufy account | Sign in, see the connection status, or sign out. |
| Camera that pops up | The one camera that pops up. The doorbell is chosen for you. |

If something stops the pop-ups working, a red **Problem** line appears at the top. Select it to fix it.

## When to pop up

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Doorbell rings | On | Pop up when someone presses the doorbell. |
| Motion detected | On | Pop up when the camera sees motion. |
| Person detected | On | Pop up when the camera sees a person. |
| Show | Over everything | Or only when nothing is playing. |
| Wait between motion pop-ups | 10 seconds | Stops repeated motion events from popping up again and again. |
| Use quiet hours | Off | No pop-ups between the times below. |
| From / Until | 23:00 to 07:00 | The quiet period. |
| Still show doorbell rings | On | Rings still pop up during quiet hours; motion doesn't. |

## When the TV is on

Used when the TV is on, including while the screensaver is showing.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Style | Picture-in-picture | Or full screen. |
| Pop-up size | 50% | How tall the pop-up is, as a share of the screen. Select it, then use left and right on the remote. Picture-in-picture only. |
| Position | Top right | Any corner. Picture-in-picture only. |
| Seconds on screen | 30 | From 5 to 120 seconds. |
| Sound | Only for doorbell rings | Or always on, or off. |

## When the TV is asleep

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Wake the TV | On | Turns the TV on for a pop-up. Off: no pop-ups while the TV is asleep. |
| Put the TV back to sleep afterwards | On | When the pop-up closes by itself, the TV goes back to sleep. Not if you close it with the remote. |
| Allow putting the TV to sleep | | Needed once for the setting above. See [Install](install.md). |
| Style, size, position, seconds, sound | Full screen | The same choices as above, used when the pop-up woke the TV. |

## All pop-ups

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Show camera name and time | On | A label on the video. |
| Back button closes the pop-up | On | While the pop-up shows, Back closes it instead of going back in the app behind it. |
| Stay up while motion continues | On | Keeps the pop-up open while the camera still sees motion. |

## Check and test

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Background service, Show over other apps, Battery and standby | Whether each is set up. |
| Show a test pop-up | Shows your camera live, with your current settings. |
| Show a test pop-up in 15 seconds | Time to open another app, start the screensaver or put the TV to sleep first. |

## Advanced

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Keep video on my home network | On | Turn off to allow Eufy's relay when a direct connection to the HomeBase fails. |
| Remind me on screen when something is wrong | Every 30 minutes | How often a problem message repeats until it's fixed. |
| Stay connected while the TV sleeps | On | Faster pop-ups after standby, for a little more power. |

**Version** at the bottom of the main screen shows the installed version. Compare it with the [latest release](https://github.com/adam-stott-dev/eufy-tv-doorbell/releases/latest).
